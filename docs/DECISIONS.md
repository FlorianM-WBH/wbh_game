# Decisions

Decisions made without human input, with their reasons.

## 2026-10-23 – Session 1 (interactive setup session, decisions made by the agent)

- **Platform: browser game, TypeScript + Vite + Phaser 3.90.** Node 22 and Google Chrome are already on the machine, so nothing needs sudo. A browser build is trivial to share with students (static `dist/`, e.g. GitHub Pages). Phaser 3.90 rather than 4.x because 3.x is mature and its API is well known; nothing in this game needs Phaser 4.
- **Architecture: pure simulation in `src/sim/` (no Phaser imports), rendering in `src/scenes/`, menus/dialogs as a DOM overlay in `src/ui/`.** The sim is deterministic (seeded RNG stored in the state) and fully unit-testable with Vitest. DOM menus are easier to style (neon CSS), to read, and to drive from automated browser tests than canvas UI.
- **Testing: Vitest for the sim (including a "balance bot" that plays 14 days and must make a profit) plus a headless-Chrome smoke test (`playwright-core` with the system Chrome, `channel: 'chrome'`) that builds, serves, plays a few scripted steps through `window.__mm` debug hooks, checks for console errors and saves screenshots.** No browser download needed.
- **Language: German UI and dialogue** (the brief is German), with 80s Denglisch slang for flavour. Code, comments and docs in English.
- **Art: all procedural pixel art generated at runtime** (canvas drawing into textures), so no external assets or licences. Game resolution 640x360, scaled up crisp (`pixelArt: true`). Cars are top-down sprites built from shape parameters per model; characters are composed from layers (skin, hair style, top, pants, shades), so every customer looks different.
- **Car brands are parodies** (Ferrero Testosterossa, Lambo Kontrach, DeLorenzo, Porschke...) to fit the over-the-top tone and avoid trademarks. Money in US dollars (Miami 1986).
- **Time model:** a day runs 08:00–20:00 game time, about 2 game minutes per real second (~6 real minutes per day). Time pauses during menus and negotiations so the player is never punished for reading jokes. Rent is charged at closing.
- **Workplaces:** office door (finances, building upgrades, save, end day), office computer (tech tree), workshop lift (repairs/upgrades; jobs run on their own, holding the action key at the lift speeds them up ×3, so the free-moving player character has a reason to be there), buy-in zone (sellers park there), sales lot (parking slots).
- **Negotiation:** each customer has a hidden limit (max pay / min accept) and patience; the player names prices or accepts the counteroffer; tactics (charm, hard, exaggerate) can each be used once per negotiation and depend on customer personality. Mood shifts the effective limit. Insulting offers cost extra patience.
- **Goal / end:** "Imperium" = every tech and building level bought (progress shown as %). Profit and stats are tracked for "maximal Gewinn".
- **Milestone plan** is kept in HANDOVER.md (M1 vertical slice done in session 1; M2 feel/content, M3 service customers, M4 deeper economy, M5 visuals, M6 balance/goals, M7 polish/release).
- **Starting conditions:** $8,000, a tired Golfo GTI and a Trabbo on the lot, rent $250/day plus $60 per building level. A balance bot is part of the unit tests so economy changes that break profitability are caught.
- **Negative money is allowed for now** (warning in the day summary). A loan-shark game-over is planned for M4 rather than now, so early balance mistakes cannot soft-lock a new player.

## 2026-10-23 – Session 2

- **Buyer wishes:** about half of the buyers wish for one specific extra that matches their taste tags (unlocked extras are 3× more likely to be wished for than locked ones, so the wish can often be met but also motivates tech). A fulfilled wish multiplies the buyer's limit by 1.15 and improves fit; a missing wish multiplies it by 0.95. Lot cars that fulfil the wish get a ★ in the customer menu. Locked wishes are labelled "(noch nicht freigeschaltet)".
- **Dialogue variety:** the pitch line is composed from the fit line + wish reaction (have/missing) or praise for a liked extra + a condition remark (wreck/poor/mint). Tactic reactions use archetype-specific lines 70% of the time (content/reactions.ts), generic lines otherwise.
- **Music:** procedural synthwave (A minor, 104 BPM: kick/snare/hats, pumping saw bass, detuned saw pad, square arpeggio with dotted-eighth echo, pentatonic lead in alternate phrases, breakdowns). Started on the first key/pointer press (browser autoplay rules). `M` mutes everything, `N` toggles music only; both persist in localStorage. Smoke test renders 8 s offline and checks level (audible, no clipping).
- **Onboarding:** a "Nächster Schritt" box (top right) shows the first unfinished of 6 core actions (buy, lift, work, sell, tech, building); progress is a bitmask in `state.tips`, so tasks can be finished in any order. `H` hides it.
- **Service customers (M3):** intent `'service'`, 20% chance whenever the buy-in zone is free and it is before 16:00. They use the buy-in zone (no seller can arrive meanwhile). One job (repair of a broken part, 65%, or an unlocked extra, 35%). Fee = (list parts cost × 1.4 + list minutes × $5) × max(0.9, archetype valuation); the player pays the parts at their (possibly discounted) price, so workshop techs raise service profit. Deadline = now + 1.8 × list minutes + 70 min (max 19:50). Early hand-over earns a tip (8% per spare hour, max 25%). Missed deadline: customer drives off with the car (from the lift if needed), no pay, parts lost. Haggling once: +25% with chance 0.35 + 0.4·charmable, otherwise 35% chance they leave. On the lift a customer car offers only the ordered job; it can be put back into the buy-in zone. Car wash/detailing deferred.

## 2026-10-23 – Session 3 (M4 deeper economy)

- **Price tags:** any lot car can get an asking price (presets 0.9/1.0/1.1/1.2/1.35 × market value, rounded to $50). Waiting buyers who have not been talked to walk up to a tagged car they like (`eyeing`), think for 18–30 game minutes, then buy at the tag price if it is at or below a stingier ceiling than at the negotiating table (`buyerLimit` with fudge 0.8–0.96 plus mood). Otherwise they say a line in a speech bubble, remember the car (`rejected`) and keep waiting; the player can still talk to them. Balance probe (14 days, seed 42): tag-only at 0.9× ≈ $55–70k profit, at 1.0× only ~9 sales, haggling bot far more. Tags are convenience, haggling earns more. A unit test guards that tag-only selling at 0.9× is profitable.
- **Reputation** (`state.rep`, 0..100, start 50, 5 tiers from "Halsabschneider" to "Legende von Miami"): sale +1, purchase +0.5, negotiation walkout −1.5, finished service +3 (+4 with tip), missed service deadline −6, service customer leaving after haggling −1, unserved customer leaving −0.25 (not if they looked at a tag). Below 50 it drifts back 15% per night. Effects: customer rate ×(0.6…1.4), start mood ±10. HUD shows 0–5 stars.
- **Loan shark** "Tony „Zinsen“ Calamari" (office → "Kredithai anrufen"): borrow $2k/$5k/$10k up to $40k, 3% interest per day added to the debt at closing, repay any time. A negative balance at closing is covered automatically with a 20% fee (so negative money no longer persists overnight). Debt over $60k at closing = game over (save deleted, "Neues Spiel"). Warnings above $40k.
- **Dealer auction** (tech "BTX-Händlerauktion", $4,000, Verkauf branch, no prerequisite): at the computer, 3 lots per day (generated on first look), any class, 30% with an extra, price 72–95% of market value + $150 fee. Exact condition only with the "Gutachter" tech, otherwise rough labels and "Marktwert unbekannt" – a deliberate gamble. Bought cars are delivered to a free slot.
- **Tip "Preisschild anbringen"** appended as the 7th first-steps tip (appended, not inserted, so the bitmask in old saves stays valid).
- Save version stays 1; all new fields are optional.

## 2026-10-23 – Session 3 (M5–M7)

- **Second lift** comes with the first workshop upgrade (level 1 "Profi-Werkstatt"), not level 2, so the cheap early building upgrade is attractive. Implemented as `state.lift` (bay 0) plus optional `state.lift2` (bay 1) instead of an array, so old saves and existing code keep working; workshop functions take an optional `bay`. "In die Werkstatt fahren" uses the first free bay. Holding E boosts only the bay the player stands at.
- **Night:** additive "light" blobs (street lamps, neon sign, ANKAUF sign, bar, windows, workshop doors) fade in from 17:00 to 19:00 on top of the existing evening tint. Four street lamps were added to the sidewalk art.
- **Customers leaving with their car** walk to it, get in, and the car drives off (scene-only; the sim removes both at once and tags the `customerLeft` event with `withCar`).
- **Back-view frames** (2, 3) for all characters, used when walking upward; side and down use the front frames.
- **Showroom detail:** level 1 shows a red display car (front view, fits the buildings' front perspective), checkered floor and a palm; level 2 adds a second car, a disco ball and neon tubes. Tuning hall (workshop 2) gets racing checkers.
- **Pacing (M6):** a human-like bot (`tests/unit/playbot.ts`: no peeking at hidden limits, takes all service jobs, repairs, tunes, buys progression when it has a reserve) finishes the empire in 20–28 game days (seeds 1–5). It serves every customer instantly, so real players should need noticeably longer, roughly 3–5 hours at ~6 real minutes per day. Costs were left unchanged; `tests/unit/longrun.test.ts` guards 15–45 days and no debt.
- **Records** (localStorage `miami-motors-86-records`): best sales profit, best company worth (cash − debt + stock cost), best deal, fastest empire day. Shown on the title screen, in the office ("Statistik & Rekorde"), on the victory screen and the day summary ("Neuer Rekord").
- **Pause menu on Esc** (only when no menu is open) with help, settings, save, back to title. **F1** opens help. Settings are keyboard-friendly: each volume (master, music, effects) steps +10% per press and wraps to 0; persisted with the existing audio prefs.
- **Deployment:** `dist/` is static with relative paths; `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys to GitHub Pages on push to main. Nothing was pushed (the origin remote exists, but publishing was not requested).
